Flagship Project

KaamKart - Worker Discovery Platform

kaamkart.in

Problem

Millions of customers struggle to find reliable, trustworthy daily-wage and contractual workers. Traditional methods involve word-of-mouth referrals, classified ads, or local contractors—all inefficient and unreliable. Workers face difficulty securing consistent work and reaching customers.

Solution

KaamKart is a comprehensive platform that bridges the gap between customers and workers. It enables customers to discover, connect, and coordinate work opportunities with vetted workers while providing workers with reliable work prospects and secure payment.

Key Features

  • ✓ Worker & Customer Registration
  • ✓ Intelligent Worker Discovery & Matching
  • ✓ Multi-language Support (Hindi & English)
  • ✓ SMS/WhatsApp Notifications
  • ✓ QR Code & UPI Payment Integration
  • ✓ Worker Availability Calendar
  • ✓ Service Request Management
  • ✓ Rating & Review System
